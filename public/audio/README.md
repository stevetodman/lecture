## Audio Sources

You can obtain murmur audio from:
- [University of Michigan Heart Sound & Murmur Library](https://open.umich.edu/find/open-educational-resources/medical/heart-sounds-murmur-library)
- [Littmann Stethoscope Sound Library](https://www.3m.com/3M/en_US/littmann-stethoscopes-us/education/heart-lung-sounds/)
- [Easy Auscultation](https://www.easyauscultation.com/)
- [The R.A.L.E. Repository](https://www.rale.ca/)

Ensure you have appropriate licensing/permissions for educational use.
