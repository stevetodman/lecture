Differential Cyanosis in Newborns

Anatomically Accurate Visual Guide

Normal

Physiologic circulation

Normal: Entire body receives oxygenated blood from LV. Small L→R flow through PDA. No differential cyanosis.

PPHN

High pulmonary pressure

PPHN: High PA pressure reverses PDA flow (R→L). Deoxygenated blood enters descending aorta. Upper body pink, lower body blue.

Critical Coarctation

Aortic narrowing

Coarctation: Narrowed aorta blocks flow to lower body. Patent DA provides R→L shunt. Upper body pink (pre-coarct), lower body blue (ductal-dependent).
