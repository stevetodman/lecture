Differential Cyanosis in Newborn

Normal • PPHN • Critical Coarctation

Normal Heart

  • Aortic P > PA P
  • L→R PDA shunt
  • Pink upper + lower

PPHN

  • High PA pressure
  • R→L PDA shunt
  • Blue lower body
  • Normal anatomy

Critical Coarctation

  • Narrowed aorta distal
  • R→L DA shunt
  • Deoxy lower body
  • Fixed obstruction
Normal: aortic pressure higher than pulmonary; L-to-R PDA; no differential cyanosis.
Systemic > pulmonary pressure → L→R PDA
PPHN: pulmonary pressure higher; R-to-L PDA; cyanotic lower body.
Pulmonary pressure stays high → R→L PDA
Critical coarctation: narrowed distal aorta; R-to-L DA; cyanotic lower body.
Distal aortic pressure low → R→L DA
