Normal Heart

All body parts receive oxygenated blood

Aorta pressure > PA pressure
PDA flow: Left → Right (small)

PPHN

High lung pressure forces blue blood to lower body

PA pressure > Aorta (reversed!)
PDA flow: Right → Left (backward)
Lower body: Blue (deoxygenated)

Critical Coarctation

Narrowed aorta blocks flow to lower body

Narrowed aorta blocks lower body flow
DA flow: Right → Left (saves lower body)
Lower body: Blue (ductal-dependent)
